Payment logos — square, transparent PNG works best (e.g. 128x128). Save as:

upi.png         ← UPI
card.png        ← Credit / Debit card
netbanking.png  ← Net banking
wallet.png      ← Wallets

.jpg, .jpeg, .webp and .png all work.
No file? The emoji from src/data/payments.js is shown instead.

Prefer a web link instead of a file? Open src/data/payments.js and paste the
link between the quotes of logo: '' for that payment method, for example:
  logo: 'https://example.com/upi-logo.png',
